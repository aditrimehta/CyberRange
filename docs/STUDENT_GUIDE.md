# Student guide

Sign in or create an account with your instructor's class code. Accept the acceptable-use policy before starting. Use the platform only for authorized educational work and only against targets inside your lab workspace.

Choose a practice lab from the catalog, or open an assigned lab from your dashboard. Nmap and SQL injection start isolated local Docker targets and expose fixed actions in the workspace. Security Log Analysis searches a bundled synthetic log. Save a draft while working, submit when ready, and review your score and explanations. The other lesson examples remain guided exercises.

Do not copy payloads or credentials from a lab to real networks. Ask your instructor if a lab behaves unexpectedly or appears to reach outside its scope.
