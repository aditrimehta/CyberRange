# Student guide

Sign in or create an account with your instructor's class code. Accept the acceptable-use policy before starting. Use the platform only for authorized educational work and only against targets inside your lab workspace.

Choose a practice lab from the catalog, or open an assigned lab from your dashboard. The guide, lab desktop, and graded questions share one workspace. Save a draft while working, submit when ready, and review your score and explanations. Stopping a session destroys its disposable environment; it does not erase submitted attempts. Sessions expire automatically when idle or at their time limit.

Do not copy payloads or credentials from a lab to real networks. Ask your instructor if a lab behaves unexpectedly or appears to reach outside its scope.
