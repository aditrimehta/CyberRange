# Student guide

Sign in or create an account with your instructor's class code. Accept the acceptable-use policy before starting. Use the platform only for authorized educational work and only against targets inside your lab workspace.

Choose one of the three practice labs, or open an assignment. The guide, interactive exercise, and questions appear one below another. Each exercise explains its learning goal and tracks completed actions on the server.

The student Overview uses the same workspace theme as teacher pages. **Continue learning** prioritizes active assignments and reattempts. Class cards, assignment cards, the Lab Catalog, and History share the same result badges, typography, and progress styles. Class and history filters help you find work without changing your saved results.

- **Nmap:** scan the target, close the listener identified by your session briefing, then rescan to verify its port is closed. The service name, port, and open-port count can vary. Keep both results for comparison.
- **SQL injection:** try a normal login, demonstrate the training bypass, then apply the parameterized query fix with the same input. The fix remains active for the rest of this session. Verify a normal login still works.
- **Security log analysis:** generate training activity first. Each new session selects its user, source address, failed-login count, and uploaded file from a training set. Use your own evidence when answering. Expand **Investigation hints** for suggested searches such as `result=failed` and `action=file_upload`. Any search that reveals the relevant evidence counts as reviewed; the suggested keywords are optional. Refreshing or resuming keeps the same incident.

Save an answer draft while working, submit when ready, and review your score. Completed actions survive a page refresh; the on-screen output history does not. Stop or submit to remove the session's environment. Closing the browser leaves it running until idle expiry. A new session starts with a fresh database and log.

Open **My classes** to see every class you have joined. Each classroom shows its own assignments, due dates, scores, and progress. Start assigned work from that class to receive credit there; catalog practice is saved separately. The **Submit answers** button is below the quiz. The pass mark is 80%; lower scores require a reattempt. Remediation remains available until you pass, even if the normal attempt allowance is used up.

**History** retains every submission, including unsuccessful attempts, with scores, feedback, and time spent. Teachers of classes you are currently enrolled in can also review your self-directed work under **Extra practice**; it does not replace their assignments. Your temporary environment is removed separately. To sign out, hover over or click your profile, choose **Sign out**, then confirm.

Do not copy payloads or credentials from a lab to real networks. Ask your instructor if a lab behaves unexpectedly or appears to reach outside its scope.
